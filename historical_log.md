# Historical Log

This file records a chronological history of all notable gardening events.
Entries should be appended to the bottom of the file, with the most
recent entry last.  Use the date headings (`### YYYY‑MM‑DD`) to
organise the log and provide context for the actions taken or
observations made.  Include details such as the plants involved,
quantities, weather conditions, tasks performed and any lessons
learned.
