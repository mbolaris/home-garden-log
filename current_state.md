## Overview (as of 2025‑08‑03)

The home garden in coastal Solana Beach, California has expanded beyond a single raised bed.  In addition to **Raised Bed 1** (planted May 18 2025 with tomatoes, peppers, eggplant and basil), there is now a **mixed tropical planter**, an in‑ground **blueberry patch**, two **3.5 ft × 5 ft VEGO planters** in the back yard, a **4 ft × 8 ft VEGO planter** and another **3.5 ft × 5 ft VEGO planter** along the south side yard, plus **three front yard planters** whose exact dimensions are still being confirmed.  Several of these containers remain unplanted while plans are finalised.  A tropical container holds pineapple, an unidentified pepper and papaya seedlings, and the blueberry patch contains blueberries, strawberries and mint.  An unplanted bed along with the new VEGO planters and front yard planters are still awaiting crops. ### Tasks & To‑Do

The following tasks represent both ongoing maintenance and specific actions identified in late July and early August.  Tick off items as you complete them and transfer completed actions with the date into `historical_log.md`.

* **Harvest and remove overripe tomatoes:** Collect split or rotting fruits from the Mortgage Lifter and Genuwine plants in Raised Bed 1; compost unusable fruit and harvest any ripe tomatoes (ongoing from July 28 – August 3).
* **Trim and preserve basil:** Cut back basil in all beds (Raised Bed 1, Left Bed and any planters) to reduce crowding; use fresh or dry the excess.
* **Remove hardened okra pod:** Cut off the old, overripe okra pod from the okra plant in Raised Bed 2 to encourage new pods.
* **Prune tomato foliage:** Thin tomato plants in both front and side beds for better airflow and disease prevention.  Stake indeterminate vines and remove suckers weekly.
* **Cut back baby’s‑breath:** Trim the ornamental baby’s‑breath around the melon patch to prevent it from crowding melon vines.
* **Improve irrigation coverage:** Adjust drip lines to ensure the edges of the melon patch and other beds receive adequate water; repair any leaks or blockages.
* **Reseed bare carrot rows:** Rake and sow seeds into the older carrot row in the back‑yard bed and the carrot strip where germination failed; keep soil consistently moist until emergence.
* **Contain and trim mint:** Top and confine the vigorous mint in the berry/blueberry planter to prevent it overtaking blueberries and strawberries.
* **Monitor milkweed for monarch activity:** Check milkweed plants daily for monarch caterpillars, eggs or chrysalises; note dates of sightings in the historical log.
* **Water deeply:** Continue to irrigate all beds and planters 2–3 times per week (more frequently for containers), adjusting for coastal heat and marine layer conditions.
* **Plan plantings for empty planters:** Decide on fall or winter crops for the vacant 2 × 7.5 ft planter, the extra VEGO planters and the front yard containers; amend soil as needed before planting.

### Weather & Environmental Notes

* Early August 2025 has been warm and dry along the Solana Beach coast, with highs in the mid‑70s to low‑80s °F and marine‑layer mornings.  There has been little to no rainfall, so irrigation is essential.
* Coastal breezes help moderate temperatures but can dry out container soil quickly; monitor moisture in the tropical planter.
