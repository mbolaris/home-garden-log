# Home Garden Log

This repository tracks the current state, history, and seed inventory of Mike Bolaris’s garden in Solana Beach, CA.

## Repository Structure

- **README.md** – This file. Describes how to use and update the repo.
- **current_state.md** – Snapshot of what is planted and growing right now.
- **historical_log.md** – Daily/periodic updates with dates, photos, and notes.
- **seed_inventory.md** – Full list of seeds on hand with purchase dates and other details.
- **/images** – Photos of planters, beds, patches, and notable plants.

## Garden Area Naming Conventions

### Backyard
- **West Planter** – 3.5' × 5' Vego, next to the shed.
- **East Planter** – 3.5' × 5' Vego, opposite side of backyard.

### South Side of House
- **4×8 Planter** – 27" Vivego plastic planter.
- **3.5×5 Pineapple/Papaya/Peppers Planter** – Contains pineapples, papayas, peppers.

### Front Yard
- **Small Plumeria/Potatoes Planter** – Smallest front yard planter; two potted plumerias, potatoes, some poppies.
- **Blueberry/Mint Planter** – ~5' × 4', 3 blueberry plants (2 Starshine, 1 Bountiful Blue) plus mint in pots.
- **Large Mixed Veg Planter** – Tomatoes (Mortgage Lifter, Genuine Beefsteak, Little Sicily, Roma, Black Beauty), peppers (Serrano, Carolina Reaper, Sweet Pepper, Golden California Wonder, Purple Beauty), basil.

### Other Areas
- **Melon Patch** – Mixed melon varieties.
- **Wildflower Strip (North Driveway)** – Wildflowers along driveway’s north side.
- **Walkway Strip (North Walkway)** – Wildflowers and other plantings.
- **Banana/Strawberry Strip (South Driveway)** – Bananas and strawberries.
- **Mailbox Patch** – Wildflowers near mailbox.

### Lawn
- Main lawn area in backyard. Sand leveling and aeration work tracked here.

## How to Update

1. **Add Photos**
   - Save photos to `/images/YYYY-MM-DD/` before committing.
   - Use descriptive names like `backyard-west-planter.jpg`.

2. **Update `current_state.md`**
   - Reflects what’s growing **today**.
   - Include variety names and conditions.

3. **Update `historical_log.md`**
   - Add new entry with today’s date.
   - Mention notable events (plantings, harvests, treatments).

4. **Update `seed_inventory.md`** as needed.

5. **Commit & Push**
   - Example commit message:  
     `Update current state and log for 2025-08-09`

## Purpose
This log is designed to preserve a complete, evolving record of the garden for reference, planning, and learning over time.
